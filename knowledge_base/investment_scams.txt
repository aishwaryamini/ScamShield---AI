INVESTMENT SCAMS

Investment scams involve misleading individuals into
investing money in fraudulent financial opportunities.

Common indicators:
- Guaranteed high returns with no risk.
- Pressure to invest immediately.
- Unverified investment platforms.
- Requests to transfer money to personal accounts.
- Fake testimonials and fabricated profit screenshots.

Safety measures:
- Verify financial intermediaries through official
  regulatory sources.
- Research investment opportunities independently.
- Avoid guaranteed-profit claims.
- Never make decisions under pressure.
