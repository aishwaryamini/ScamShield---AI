FAKE JOB OFFER SCAMS

Job scams involve fraudulent recruitment offers designed
to obtain money or personal information from job seekers.

Common indicators:
- Requests for registration or interview fees.
- Guaranteed employment without a proper selection process.
- Unverified recruiter identities.
- Unrealistic salary promises.
- Requests for sensitive identity or banking information.

Safety measures:
- Verify recruiters through official company websites.
- Check whether the job opening exists on the official
  careers page.
- Avoid paying suspicious recruitment fees.
- Never share sensitive financial credentials.
