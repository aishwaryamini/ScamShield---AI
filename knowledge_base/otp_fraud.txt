OTP AND BANKING FRAUD

OTP fraud involves social engineering techniques that
persuade individuals to disclose one-time passwords.

Common indicators:
- Requests for OTP through calls or messages.
- Claims that an account will be blocked immediately.
- Fake bank representatives requesting credentials.
- Unexpected account verification links.
- Requests to disclose banking passwords.

Safety measures:
- Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
- Do not trust unsolicited banking calls.
- Verify account-related issues using official bank channels.
- Report unauthorized transactions immediately.
