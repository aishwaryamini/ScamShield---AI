UPI PAYMENT FRAUD

UPI fraud involves deceptive techniques used to steal money
or obtain sensitive payment information through digital
payment systems.

Common indicators:
- Requests to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.
- Fake payment screenshots.
- Unknown collect requests.
- Fraudulent QR codes.
- Impersonation of customer support representatives.

Safety measures:
- A UPI PIN is generally required to authorize sending money,
  not to receive money.
- Verify transactions in your official payment application.
- Do not approve unknown collect requests.
- Never share your UPI PIN.
- Contact your bank through official channels.
