PHISHING ATTACKS

Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers impersonate
trusted organizations or individuals to steal sensitive
information.

Common indicators:
- Unexpected messages containing suspicious links.
- Requests for passwords or banking credentials.
- Threatening account suspension.
- Urgent requests to verify personal information.
- Fake websites impersonating legitimate organizations.

Safety measures:
- Verify website addresses before entering information.
- Avoid clicking unexpected links.
- Never disclose passwords or OTPs.
- Contact organizations through official channels.
